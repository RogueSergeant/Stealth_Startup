Questions You Might Have

Is this like parental control, but for adults?

Not quite. Parental control cards have someone else approving your purchases. With Impulse Card, you set the rules and you justify purchases. It's about giving yourself structure and accountability, not having someone else control your money. Think of it as a financial accountability partner, not a parent.

What if I need to make an emergency purchase?

We're designing multiple override paths. You can adjust your rules anytime, approve specific merchants permanently, and there'll be an option for human review within 24 hours. The goal is to stop impulse spending, not block legitimate needs.

Will my card get declined in front of people? That's embarrassing.

The decline message is generic—same as any normal card decline. Nobody knows it's because of budget rules. You'll get a discreet notification on your phone to open the app and explain why you need it. Most approvals happen in under 10 seconds, so you can retry immediately.

What if I just use my other bank card instead?

That's the key question. This only works if you use it as your primary spending card. In our research, 73% of ADHD adults said they would because the accountability is what they're looking for. But you're right—it requires commitment. That's why we're offering a free trial period when we launch.

How much does it cost?

We're planning £15/month. In our research, people with ADHD lose an average of £50-150 monthly to impulse spending. If this saves you even £50/month, it pays for itself 3x over. We'll offer a 1-month free trial so you can see the savings before paying.

When will this launch?

We're currently in the research phase (hence this waitlist). If we get enough interest and can prove it works with a pilot group, we're aiming for launch in 6-9 months. Everyone on the waitlist will get early access.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We take privacy seriously. Your spending data is encrypted, never sold, and only used to make approval decisions. We're GDPR compliant from day one. You can request to delete all your data at any time.

What banks do you work with?

We'll be a prepaid Mastercard or Visa card through a licensed partner, so it works everywhere cards are accepted. You can top it up from any UK bank account.
