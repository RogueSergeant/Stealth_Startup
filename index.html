Questions You Might Have

Won't this be embarrassing in public?

The decline looks like any normal card decline (insufficient funds, etc.). You get a discreet phone notification. Nobody knows it's because of budget rules. Most approvals happen in 10 seconds, so you can retry immediately at the same shop. No awkwardness, no explanation needed.

What if I just game the AI with the right words?

We track patterns. If your justifications always use similar language and always get approved, it triggers human review. But honestly, if you're taking the time to write a thoughtful justification, you're already pausing to think—which is the point. Even "gaming" the system creates the friction that prevents true impulse spending.

Can my partner/spouse see my spending?

No. This is your personal accountability partner, not shared monitoring. Only you see your justifications and decisions. If you want to share access, you can, but it's not default. Your spending is private.

Is this like parental control, but for adults?

Not quite. Parental control cards require someone else to approve your purchases. With Impulse Card, you set the rules and you approve your own purchases. It's a financial accountability partner, not a parent. You stay in control—we just make you pause first.

What if I need to make an emergency purchase?

Instant override in the app, or approve specific merchants permanently (pharmacy, hospital, etc.). There's also an "urgent—review later" option that approves immediately but prompts reflection afterwards. We stop impulses, not emergencies.

What if I just use my other bank card instead?

Then this won't work. You need to make Impulse Card your primary card. In our research, 73% said they'd do exactly that because they're desperate for external accountability. This is for people who are tired of relying on willpower that never shows up.

How much does it cost?

£15/month after a 1-month free trial. Our research shows people lose £50-200/month to impulse spending. If this saves you just £50/month, it pays for itself 3x over.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your spending data is encrypted, never sold, and only used for approval decisions. GDPR compliant from day one. You can delete all your data anytime with one click.
