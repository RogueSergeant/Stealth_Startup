How It Works

Not a budget tracker. A spending blocker.

1

Set Your Rules Once

One-time setup: "Block purchases over £40 unless I justify them. Groceries unlimited. Block ASOS and Amazon after 10pm."

2

Card Blocks Automatically

Try to buy that £89 keyboard at 11pm? Declined. You get a notification: "Why do you need this right now?" Open the app to justify it.

3

AI Evaluates in 10 Seconds

Type your reason. AI checks it against your goals and spending patterns. Result: approved immediately, delayed 7 days for reflection, or kindly declined with a reason why.

Why I'm Building This

AR

Alfie Roberts

Software Engineer • MSc Applied AI

Last year, I lost £1,623 to impulse purchases I regretted within 24 hours.

£89 for a mechanical keyboard I used twice. Three Kindle books I never read. A £140 "productivity course" I never started. Hobby after hobby, each one "definitely the thing this time."

I have ADHD. Executive function challenges mean that in the moment, my brain genuinely believes every purchase is essential. By the time I realise it wasn't, the money's gone.

I tried everything: Monzo pots, YNAB budgets, hiding my cards, deleting shopping apps. None of it worked because they all rely on me remembering to check them before I buy something. But when I see something I want, I forget those tools exist.

I needed something that didn't rely on my memory or willpower. Something that would physically stop me and make me explain myself before I could complete the purchase.

So I'm building the card I needed. One that prevents overspending before it happens, not just tells you about it afterwards.

I'm sharing this work-in-progress because I strongly suspect I'm not the only one who needs this. If you've ever felt that same frustration - knowing you need to stop, but being unable to in the moment - this is for you too.

Questions You Might Have

This sounds great, but does it actually exist yet?

No. This is a pre-launch landing page. I'm validating demand before building. If enough people want this, I'll build it. If 10 people sign up, I'll know this isn't the solution people need.

Won't this be embarrassing in public?

The decline looks exactly like any other card decline. Your notification is discreet. The approval takes 10 seconds in the app. No one watching knows you're justifying a purchase.

What if I just game the AI with the right words?

You might. But here's the thing: even if you learn to write justifications that always work, you're still creating friction. You're still pausing. That pause is often enough to break the impulse. If you're deliberately gaming it, you probably didn't need the card in the first place.

Can my partner/spouse see my spending?

No. This is personal accountability, not joint accountability. If you want to share access with someone, that's an optional feature we might build, but it's not the default.

Is this like parental control, but for adults?

No. You set the rules. You approve your own purchases. The AI isn't a parent - it's an accountability partner holding you to your own standards. Big difference.

What if I need to make an emergency purchase?

There'll be an instant override in the app. You can also permanently approve specific merchants. Or use the "urgent - review later" option that approves immediately but flags for review.

What if I just use my other bank card instead?

You probably will sometimes. This only works if you commit to using it as your primary card. It's not for casual use. It's for people who are desperate enough for external accountability that they're willing to lock themselves in.

How much will this actually cost?

When will this actually launch?

Pilot programme Q1 2026 (100 users). Public launch Q2 2026. Those dates might slip. I'm building this properly, not rushing it.

Is my data actually safe?

Yes. Encrypted, never sold, built for GDPR compliance from day one. You can delete everything with one click. Want to see what data I have? Just ask.

What if this just doesn't work for me?

Then you cancel. 1-month free trial. No long-term contract. If it doesn't help you, I don't want your money.
