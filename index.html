How Impulse Card Works

Traditional banking apps tell you after you've spent. We stop you before. It's prevention, not tracking.

1

Set Your Boundaries

Define approved categories, merchants, and monthly budgets. Your everyday essentials stay frictionless.

2

Real-Time Protection

Transactions outside your rules are blocked instantly. You receive a notification to justify the purchase.

3

AI Evaluation

Explain why you need this purchase. Our AI considers your budget, patterns, and goals before approving.

4

Intentional Spending

High-value purchases get a 7-day cooling-off period. If you still want it after a week, go for it.
